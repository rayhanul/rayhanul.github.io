## Accurate and Private Multi-Camera Surveillance System on Time-Sensitive Networks
accent: #48c774
tag: NSF-Funded Research Project
PhD Research, University of Connecticut · Fall 2025 – Present

Designing and evaluating TSN scheduling and runtime reconfiguration for deterministic, low-jitter communication across a privacy-aware multi-camera edge-intelligence system; built a TSN edge-AI testbed (TSN switch + Raspberry Pi 4 nodes).
